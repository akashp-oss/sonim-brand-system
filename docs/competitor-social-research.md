# Competitor social research: rugged devices and public-safety comms

Date: 2026-09-25. This was a short scan (about 20 minutes). Most social platforms and several blogs
(tribalimpact.com, shortyawards.com) blocked direct fetching. The findings below combine search-result
snippets, press and newsroom pages, and general knowledge of how these brands post. Treat them as
patterns, not measured data.

## Observations per competitor

**Zebra Technologies**
- Runs the #RuggedLife series: personal, inspirational profiles of people who work in tough conditions. This is the "in the field" customer story format.
- Promotes thought-leadership blog content ("Your Edge") on LinkedIn, X, Facebook and Instagram. Its Instagram account is nearly dormant, so LinkedIn is the main channel.
- Launch posts show the product with its feature callouts, usually on a plain background.

**Motorola Solutions** (Instagram: "Solving for safer")
- Covers launches such as APX NEXT and ViQi, and runs booth and demo posts for trade shows (IACP, APCO, IWCE) with the booth number and dates.
- Posts about the Motorola Solutions Foundation and its community work (volunteer hours, grants, National Night Out) to build culture and employer brand.
- Gives first-responder stories and "when every second counts" messages the most weight.

**Kyocera (DuraForce)**
- Proves durability through third-party torture-test videos, such as JerryRigEverything and a motorcycle run-over. "Still works" is the payoff line.
- Supports the videos with spec rows: MIL-STD-810H, IP68 and drop height.

**Samsung (Galaxy XCover / Tab Active)**
- Runs "Galaxy for Work" short films by sector (public safety, finance), plus partner announcements with Microsoft and Verizon for frontline workers.
- Posts drop-and-IP spec callouts (IP68, 1.5 m drop, MIL-STD) and product-in-hand shots with a programmable side key.

**Cat phones (Bullitt)**
- Features one hero capability (the FLIR thermal camera) with split before/after images: a visible photo next to the thermal view.
- Uses job-site photography with the product in hand, plus user-generated content from tradespeople.

**Panasonic TOUGHBOOK**
- Runs LinkedIn-led social selling through an employee ambassador programme. Posts cover partner news (SentinelOne, Lenovo), new-hire welcomes, sales kick-offs and case studies.
- Uses customer-story cards for defence and emergency-response users.

**Crosscall**
- Instagram (about 23K followers) mixes lifestyle and outdoor photography with guarantee and battery messages (5-year guarantee, IP68).
- Posts "rugged vs. performance" myth-busting blog content that it cuts into carousels.

**Getac**
- Merged its LinkedIn pages into one "Getac" page. Posts police and sheriff success stories (Wichita PD, Wake County) as customer-proof cards.

**Honeywell**
- Posts are spec-driven, such as the CT47: drop height in feet, tumble count and battery mAh. Webinar and campaign landing-page promotion is common.

**FirstNet / FirstNet Authority**
- Posts first-responder testimonials with a strong pull quote. Also covers event recaps, deployable assets in action and "stress test" retrospectives.

**Cross-industry B2B pattern (LinkedIn document carousels)**
- 4:5 PDF carousels outperform single images on LinkedIn. The cover should hook in under two seconds, each inner slide should hold one idea, and the last slide should carry the CTA.

## Post type → competitor practice → Sonim template

| Post type | What competitors do | Sonim template (id) |
|---|---|---|
| Spec callout / feature annotation | Zebra, Honeywell and Samsung show the product with its key features labelled | `ig-spec`: XP5plus 5G render with leader lines and red markers |
| Durability proof | Kyocera and Cat use torture-test videos; Honeywell and Samsung use drop and IP spec rows | `ig-durability`: IP68, IPx9K and MIL-STD-810H as large ratings with plain-language meanings |
| Numbered list ("3 reasons") | Common across B2B feeds | `ig-reasons`: three reasons, each with a red number, title and sourced detail |
| Testimonial with portrait | FirstNet, Getac and Zebra #RuggedLife | `ig-testimonial`: portrait, name plate and quote placeholder |
| Customer proof number | Getac and Panasonic case-study cards | `so-proof`: GMR's 1,200 XP8 deployment in a Rugged Container over photography |
| Partner announcement | Samsung with Microsoft and Verizon; Panasonic with SentinelOne and Lenovo | `ig-partner`: red square with the Sonim + partner lockup |
| Launch teaser | Motorola APX NEXT reveals | `ig-teaser`: partial product reveal cropped by the frame, with a date |
| Product lineup | Portfolio posts from Zebra and Samsung | `ig-lineup`: XP5plus 5G, MegaConnect and Klein VALOR RSM tiles |
| Tip / how-to | Software explainers | `ig-howto`: SonimWare Cloud, SCOUT and Kodiak dispatch |
| Hiring / culture | Motorola Foundation; Panasonic new-hire posts | `ig-hiring`: team photo with a list of open roles |
| Carousel (cover, inner slides, end) | LinkedIn document posts from all B2B brands | `carousel-cover`, `carousel-compare` (sourced before/after-style comparison), `carousel-points`, `carousel-end` |
| LinkedIn document cover | Guides and e-books as PDF carousels | `so-doc-cover`: 75% red Expanded Notch with a photo strip |
| Event countdown | Motorola and FirstNet trade-show booth posts | `story-countdown`: large day count, event and booth details |
| Poll / "this or that" | Common engagement story on Instagram | `story-poll`: two option tiles, with clear space for the poll sticker |

Existing templates that already cover other types: `ig-event` and `so-webinar` (event and speaker
cards), `ig-quote` and `so-x` (quote cards), `ig-stat` and `story-stat` (a single number),
`ig-product` and `story-product` (launch).
