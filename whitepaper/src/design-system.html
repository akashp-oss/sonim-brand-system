Five rules for every white paper
  1. Four colors, used boldly. Sonim Red, Black, Gray and White. Red is for emphasis, the notch and one hero panel per spread, not for body text.
  2. Sentence case, flush left. Never ALL CAPS or Title Case, including labels and table headers. Headlines end with a period when they are statements.
  3. Headlines in black, key phrase in red. On red panels the emphasis flips to black.
  4. The notch anchors every page headline. One notch per page at the left edge, the same size on every page.
  5. Proof over claims. Every statistic carries a red superscript source number that resolves on the back cover.

Standard page zones
  1. Header zone. Label (8 pt red) + H1, with the notch aligned to the label’s top.
  2. Lead zone. One lead paragraph across 10–12 columns.
  3. Content zone. 7 + 5 columns (text + sidebar), 6 + 6 (case studies) or 12 (tables, stacks).
  4. Footer zone. Logo bottom-left only. No page numbers in the footer; the folio sits in the running header.
White paper design system/ 06
Graphic elements

All three elements come from one shape: the notch, built at an 18:1 ratio with 26.5° angled ends. X = notch width = 9 pt (0.125 in); notch height = 18X = 162 pt (2.25 in).

Keep the notch the same size on every page. Scale only in 25% steps.

White paper design system/ 09
Imagery & icons

Authentic situations. Natural light. Real people with quirks. Evidence of work. Sense of place. Rugged toughness. Clear and direct compositions.

Product renders sit on Sonim Gray or white with natural shadows. Icons are black line drawings on a 24 × 24 grid with one red detail.

Do
  • Use one Display title and one H1 per page.
  • Keep the notch the same size on every page.
  • Put the key phrase of each headline in red.
  • Bleed photography off the page edge; keep type inside margins.
  • Source every number; list sources on the back cover.
  • Show partner logos in single-color black, only when approved.
Don’t
  • Set anything in ALL CAPS or Title Case.
  • Add colors, gradients, drop shadows or tints outside the palette.
  • Use more than one red hero panel per page.
  • Stretch, recolor or add effects to the logo; min width 0.5 in.
  • Use staged, over-lit or heavily filtered stock photos.
  • Make a Rugged Container wider than it is tall.
White paper design system/ 10
Making a new white paper

Files: sonim-whitepaper.css (tokens and components), whitepaper-template.html (T1–T5 masters with placeholders), sonim-logo.svg, and an images folder.

  1. Brief to outline. Map the brief to T1–T5. Four content pages is the default; add T2 or T3 pages in pairs.
  2. Write to the word budgets on page 08. Headlines in sentence case with one red key phrase.
  3. Collect proof. Every stat gets a source number; customer quotes need approval.
  4. Pick photography per the imagery rules: one people photo on T1, customer photos on T3, product renders on T4, one full-bleed photo on T5.
  5. Build. Duplicate whitepaper-template.html, replace placeholders, point image slots at your files. The logo is injected at build time.
  6. Render and check. Export to PDF at US Letter with backgrounds on. Check overflow on each page, notch alignment, and that nothing is set in caps.
  7. Hand off. For InDesign production, rebuild using the same tokens: paragraph styles named Display, H1, H2, H3, Label, Lead, Body, Small, Stat, Quote; swatches Sonim Red, Black, Gray; Akzidenz-Grotesk Pro throughout.
