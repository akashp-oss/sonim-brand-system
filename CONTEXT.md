## 1. What this project is

A brand design system for **Sonim Technologies** (rugged phones and connectivity for first
responders and industry; *Sonim, a NEXA company* since January 2026), produced by the design studio
**Bang Design**.

It has two parts:

1. **Brand template library** (`library/`): one self-contained HTML file with 55 editable
   templates across 10 sets (white papers, presentation, Instagram posts, carousels and stories, social graphics,
   posters, roll-up banners, web/display ads, stationery). Built so an **HTML to Figma plugin**
   (for example html.to.design) can import every artboard with editable text and separate layers.
2. **EMS white paper system** (`whitepaper/`): the first Sonim white paper, *Connected care in
   motion*, plus its design system, a blank template, a Word content version and a Figma-style
   in-browser editor. The white paper is the **visual foundation** the library was derived from.

Everything is plain HTML/CSS, generated by small Python/Node scripts. No framework.

## 4. Brand rules (non-negotiable)

Source of truth: **Sonim Visual Identity Standards & Brand Guidelines (1 March 2020)** and
**Sonim Social Media General Use Templates (2023)**. Both are in `reference/`
(client-confidential; keep this repo private):
`reference/Sonim_BrandGuidelines_2020.pdf` (original, 36 MB) and
`reference/Sonim_SocialMediaGuidelines_2023.pdf` (compressed from the 440 MB Illustrator export to
7 MB; Illustrator editing data removed, all 46 pages intact). Everything below was extracted from them.

### Colour (four colours only, no tints or tones)
| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK | Pantone |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sonim Red | `#CF102D` | 207 16 45 | 12 100 91 3 | 186 C |
| Sonim Black | `#000000` | 0 0 0 | 0 0 0 100 | Black |
| Sonim Gray | `#E5ECEE` | 229 236 238 | 9 3 4 0 | 649 (70%) |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 255 255 255 | 0 0 0 0 | — |

- Approved pairs (fg on bg): black / red / white on gray; black / red on white (gray on white for
  graphics only); black / white / gray on red; white / gray on black.
- **Never:** red *type* on black, gray *type* on white, tints of brand colours, red tint over photos,
  off-palette colours. Avoid compositions that are too dark and heavy.
- Hairlines: Sonim Gray on white, white on gray.

### Typography
- **Akzidenz Grotesk** Bold + Regular (Berthold). Condensed cuts only for spec tables.
- **Arial** is the approved fallback (Office, email, and this HTML build — Akzidenz is not licensed
  on the studio machine). Do not use any other font.
- **Sentence case** always. Never ALL CAPS or Title Case (labels, table headers and buttons included).
- Flush left. Headlines graphic and impactful: tight kerning, tight leading.
- Headline pattern: **black, with the key phrase in Sonim Red, ending with a period**
  ("Connected care **in motion.**"). On red backgrounds the emphasis turns black.

### Logo
- Single colour only: black on light, white on dark/photo/red. Red on white/gray is used in the
  social guidelines and is allowed sparingly.
- Clear space = height of two sets of connectivity lines on every side.
- Minimum 0.5 in (print) / 50 px (digital).
- When it is not the hero: **bottom-left or bottom-right on the margin**. Inside a graphic element,
  equal margins. Vertical (rotated) logo on red is the event/roll-up treatment.
- Partner lockup: Sonim + partner, no tagline, both single colour.

### Graphic elements (all derived from one shape: the notch)
- **Notch**: 18:1 ratio, 26.5° angled ends (rise of 2X over run of X). Standalone notch anchors the
  headline on the **left edge**, size consistent across a document, ≤ ⅓ of the longest side.
  Brandbook standard: 2.25 in × 0.125 in; notch inside a container: 3.375 in × 0.1875 in (< tabloid).
  Scale only in 25% steps.
- **Rugged Container**: rectangle with a right notch 2X from the top and a left notch 2X from the
  bottom; neither passes the centre line. Always **taller than wide**, height ≤ 175% of width.
  Live area inset equally (2X). Holds copy, data, logo.
- **Expanded Notch**: panel from the left edge covering 25 / 50 / 75% of the width, extending to three
  edges, with the notch protruding from its right edge. Red or gray. May be rotated 90° for very
  narrow formats (roll-ups, stories).

### Photography and iconography
- Authentic situations, natural light, real people with quirks, evidence of work, sense of place,
  rugged toughness, clear and direct compositions. Product renders on gray/white with natural
  shadows. No staged, over-lit, filtered or red-tinted images.
- Icons: line icons, black with one red detail; single white on black if unavoidable.

### Voice
Personality: confident, genuine, informative, passionate. Tone: positive, optimistic, grounded,
smart. Taglines: *Built for the extraordinary. Designed to endure. Trusted to deliver.*

## 5. The system (decisions made on top of the brandbook)

The white paper established the proportions; every other format re-derives them:

- **Margin M ≈ 7.4% of the artboard's short side** (white paper: 45 pt on 612 pt).
- **Notch unit X = M ÷ 5**; notch = X × 18X; placed at x = 0, top aligned to the label's top.
- Hierarchy on every artboard: **label** (small, bold, red) → **headline** (bold, tight) →
  lead/body → logo on the margin.
- Settings: product on Sonim Gray; messages inside a Rugged Container over photography; single
  sourced numbers on Sonim Red; red Expanded Notch to divide photo and type; giant red model number
  ("XP5") with the product render overlapping it (from the brandbook's XP3 examples).

| Format | Size (px) | M | X | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| White paper page | 816 × 1056 (US Letter) | 60 | 12 | 12-col grid, 14 pt gutter in the PDF build |
| Presentation | 1920 × 1080 | 120 | 24 | logo bottom-left 150 px wide |
| Instagram post | 1080 × 1350 / 1080 × 1080 | 80 | 16 | logo 160–180 px |
| Instagram story | 1080 × 1920 | 80 | 16 | safe zone: nothing important in top 250 / bottom 340 px |
| Social landscape | 1200 × 627, 1600 × 900, 1584 × 396 | 64 | 12–16 | LinkedIn cover: keep lower-left clear |
| Poster | 1200 × 1600 (18×24 in), 1200 × 1800 (24×36 in) | 96 | 20 | |

| Stationery | 1200×400 email header, 1050×600 card, 600×160 signature, 1920×1080 virtual bg | 64 | 12–24 | |

## 6. Brand template library — how it works and how to change it

- `templates.py` builds each artboard from `gen.py` primitives with absolute coordinates in px.
  Text uses `**…**` for the red key phrase and `\n` for line breaks:
  ```python
  text('Headline', 'Built for the\n**worst days.**', 'h1', BLACK, 88)
  page_notch(X, top)                 # notch on the left edge
  rugged('Name', l, t, w, h, X, WHITE, content_html)   # Rugged Container
  xnotch('Name', l, t, w, h, X, RED, inner_html)       # Expanded Notch
  logo(width, WHITE, l=M, b=M)       # single-colour logo on the margin
  photo('Name', 'crew', l, t, w, h, '40% 50%')         # key = file in library/src/img
  F.append(('ig', artboard('ig-new', 'Instagram post · Name', 1080, 1350, GRAY, html, 'use', 'post')))
  ```
- Add a set: add a tuple to `SECTION_META`. Add an image: drop it in `library/src/img/`
  (JPEG ≤ 1600 px, or quantised PNG for cut-outs) and reference it by file name without extension.
- Rebuild: `python3 library/src/build.py` → `library/dist/…html`. QA: `node library/src/shots.js`
  then `node library/src/qa.js` (reports text colours, fonts, broken images, overflow).
- Images are stored once in a JSON block and assigned to `<img data-img>` at load time, which keeps
  the file small; "Save file" strips them again before saving.

### App features (the UI chrome, never exported)
Fit / 25 / 50 / 100% zoom · **Guides** (margins, story safe zones) · **Edit** (type into any text,
double-click an image to replace it) · **Save file** (self-contained copy) ·
**Figma import view** (true size, no UI; also via `?figma` in the URL; Esc to exit) ·
**Hover tools** on every template: *Copy SVG* (paste straight into Figma), *Copy for Figma*
(JSON for `figma-plugin/`, rebuilt with auto layout), *Download SVG* · brand-rules panel with
click-to-copy colour chips · sidebar becomes a drawer under 980 px.

How the exporters work (`export.js`): the artboard is measured at 100%. Fills become rects,
photos become clipped `<image>`s honouring object-fit/position, inline SVGs become transformed
groups, and text is measured per character into `<tspan>`s at exact baselines. `data-name` becomes
the layer name. For Figma JSON every `display:flex` becomes an auto-layout frame (gap, padding,
alignment, `flex:1` → fill). Uneven spacing from margins becomes explicit *Spacer* frames, and
`.grid2` becomes a wrapping horizontal layout.

### Figma import rules (keep these when editing)
- Every visible thing is a real element: text in `.tx` divs, photos as `<img>`, logo and notches as
  inline SVG, fills as divs. **No pseudo-elements, no CSS background images, no canvas.**
- Every element has `data-name` (layer name). Groups nest logically: artboard → background /
  photo / graphic elements / content stack / logo.
- Stacks use flexbox (plugins usually convert these to auto layout).
- Run the plugin **in Figma import view**. Whether `data-name` becomes the layer name depends on the
  plugin; the structure is preserved either way. Not yet tested inside Figma.

## 7. White paper system

- Source: `whitepaper/src/Sonim_EMS_Whitepaper.html` (+ `assets/sonim-whitepaper.css`).
  PDF: `make whitepaper` (Playwright; **Arial must be installed**, otherwise Chromium substitutes
  Liberation Sans — check with `pdffonts`, only ArialMT / Arial-BoldMT should appear).
- Editor: `make editor` builds a self-contained HTML (CSS and images inlined, `editor2.js` appended).
  Features: pages + layers panels, click/double-click/Ctrl-click selection like Figma, drag with
  smart snapping, resize handles, align / distribute, text alignment, brand-only colour swatches,
  brand checks (min 6.5 pt, caps, off-brand colour), undo/redo, **auto layout** (vertical moves push
  content below), **locking** (header, footnotes, logo, closing band locked by default), overflow
  warning, save via file picker, export PDF (Letter, margins none, background graphics on).
- `editor-export.pdf` is the client's latest layout (made in the editor). To merge those tweaks,
  open the editor, redo them, **Save file**, and replace `src/Sonim_EMS_Whitepaper.html` with the
  saved page's content (strip the editor script) — or ask for a diff against the PDF.

on ambulance/EMS communications using Sonim customer proof points (GMR, Star EMS). Four-page

"communications as a radio problem" to "an operational mobility platform".

**Decisions:** title option 1 *Connected care in motion* (sentence case per brand). Writing style

## 10. Guardrails for anyone (or any model) changing this

- Stay inside the four colours; no tints, gradients, drop shadows, or new colours.
- Sentence case, flush left, headline = black + red key phrase + period.
- One notch per artboard/page, left edge, top aligned to the label, size = 18X with X = M ÷ 5.
- Rugged Containers taller than wide; Expanded Notch covers 25/50/75%.
- Logo single colour on the margin, ≥ 50 px wide.
- Never invent statistics, quotes, customers or product specs. Reuse the facts table (§8) with its
  source, or leave a clearly marked placeholder.
- Keep the library Figma-friendly: real elements, `data-name` on everything, no pseudo-elements or
  background images, flexbox for stacks.
- Don't commit fonts (licensed). Keep the repo private: it contains the client brand guideline PDFs.
- After changes: rebuild, run the QA scripts, and check `pdffonts` shows only Arial.
